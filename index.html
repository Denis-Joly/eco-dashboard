    The mental model

    A regime is a pattern, not a number

    Every family answers a different question. The interpretation becomes stronger when independent families confirm the same story.

    VOLATILITY & INTERNALS

    How large, and how synchronized?

    Index volatility, constituent volatility, dispersion and correlation reveal whether moves reinforce or cancel one another.

    BREADTH & CREDIT

    How widely is the story spreading?

    Breadth tests participation. Credit and funding conditions test whether stress has escaped the equity-options complex.

    RATES, CROSS-ASSET & MACRO

    What is transmitting the regime?

    Rates, the dollar and growth data help distinguish a local rotation from a broader inflation, liquidity or economic shock.

    A useful question

    “Does the same story appear across markets, or only in one layer?”

    One unusual indicator is context. Several independent indicators moving coherently are evidence. The dashboard should always show which signals support a regime and which ones contradict it.

    Methodology & limits

    Transparent by design

    Hosted observations come from public sources and retain their own release cadence, revision policy and comparison window. Optional Cboe files are processed only in the current browser session.

    Source: Office of Financial Research, OFR Financial Stress Index. Checked each weekday; the source is published with a two-business-day lag.

    How is the percentile calculated?

    It is the midrank of the latest value among valid observations in the stated comparison window: observations below it plus half of equal observations, divided by the total. A 90th percentile is a historical rank, not a 90% probability of a future event.

    How will daily and slower economic data be combined?

    Market closes, weekly releases and monthly observations will keep separate timestamps. A regime can use the latest information that was genuinely available on its evaluation date, without pretending that every series updates together.

    How should “typical” be understood?

    “Typical” means within the central half of the selected historical sample. It does not mean safe, permanent, or guaranteed to continue. Fixed rules such as “VIX 16 is normal” lose important context.

    What happens when I open a local CSV?

    The file is validated and converted to chart data in this browser tab. The latest value, historical rank and summary statistics are calculated locally. The dashboard does not send the file or its contents anywhere, and it does not retain them after a reload.

    Are the public stress indicators substitutes for VIX or dispersion?

    No. OFR categories measure broad financial stress and its contributions. They do not reproduce option-implied index volatility, constituent volatility, dispersion or correlation. The two layers answer different questions and become more informative when interpreted together.

    What are the interpretation limits?

    The regime summary is deterministic educational commentary based on relative historical ranks. It is not a forecast, trading recommendation, or substitute for independent financial analysis.
